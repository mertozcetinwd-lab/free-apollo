# Features

| Area | Current behavior | Source |
|---|---|---|
| People and companies | Local records, CSV import, search, filters, saved views, editable detail pages | Built-in record engine |
| Discovery | Public company and local business search; chosen company page leader candidates; optional capped people search on an owner's key | Wikidata, OpenStreetMap, official company websites, optional treg |
| Lists | Named people or company groups with explicit membership | This app |
| Contact evidence | Field source, HTTPS link and observation date visible when supplied; absent dates stay unknown | Search results or sourced imports |
| Enrichment | Capped work email finding and verification with unknown and risky states | This app, optional treg |
| Sequences | Ordered email, call, action and manual LinkedIn drafts, minute/hour/day delays, variants, schedule, safety rules, planned people | This app |
| Message draft | First message with a cited company observation or visible placeholder, then save as a draft sequence | This app |
| Tasks and pipeline | Tasks, notes, deals, board, stages, activity | Built-in record engine |
| Analytics | Local coverage, pipeline, actual cost ledger | This app |

The current app does not send messages or connect a mailbox. A sourced records file imports the fields listed in [TRANSFER.md](../TRANSFER.md). A supplied source date is observed evidence; an absent date is unknown. A separate provider verdict is needed to call an email verified. Free Apollo needs no Free Clay or Free CRM installation.
