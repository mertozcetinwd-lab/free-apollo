# Logged-in Apollo reanalysis

Date: 2026-09-30, owner's trial workspace. This pass followed the owner's correction that Free Apollo must be a standalone product. It records what the live UI exposed, not a claim that every feature was functionally exercised. No mailbox was connected, sequence activated, message sent, call made or purchase completed. An empty sequence named `Free Apollo teardown demo` was created to inspect the editor. It has zero steps and zero contacts and remains inactive in the Apollo workspace because its More menu did not open during cleanup.

## Search and records

- People search exposes Total, Net New and Saved modes, recent searches, saved views and a large filter catalogue. The categories cover person, company, engagement, source and miscellaneous attributes. The live rows are Apollo data, not data the rebuild can redistribute.
- Company search exposes 30+ filters and a separate result table. In a zero-credit check for the later local-service comparison, exact-domain searches returned one matching row each for Riverbend Electric, Gator Air & Energy and Bertie Heating & Air Conditioning. An exact-domain search for Hogle & Son returned no row in this view. That is a search result, not proof the company is absent from Apollo.
- Lists and Saved People/Companies are separate surfaces. Enrichment shows data health, CSV/CRM pathways and waterfall options, with quoted credit rules in the account pricing panel. No new reveal or enrichment was run in this pass.

## Sequence builder

- Creating a manual sequence offered a name, description, business-hours schedule and advanced ruleset, folder and tag choices. The weekday 8 AM to 5 PM window appeared as a default.
- The step menu included automatic email, manual email, phone call, action item, LinkedIn connection, LinkedIn message, profile view and post interaction. A manual email step exposed AI assisted, prompt and template modes, A/B testing, timing controls, subject and body fields, and personalization. The unsaved step was discarded.
- A saved sequence has Editor, Contacts, Activity, Health, Report and Settings tabs. Contacts has status groupings; Settings exposes schedule, ruleset, owner, tags and folder. The default ruleset includes reply, out-of-office, meeting, unsubscribe, connected call, bounce and spam stops, plus daily sending limits. Empty tabs do not demonstrate working deliverability or reporting.
- Apollo showed no linked mailboxes. The empty demo sequence could not activate. No outbound message was sent.

## Other areas revisited

- Workflows showed templates and filters. Analytics showed dashboard, report and goal areas, but there was no activity to verify metrics. Website Visitors required site setup. Forms offered a form builder and enrichment path. These controls were observed without connecting a site, creating a form or running a workflow.
- The account displayed 174 credits at the beginning of this pass and 224 after Apollo displayed a 50-credit onboarding reward. The balance increase matched that reward. No charged action was run here. The earlier measured 11-credit pass remains separate.

## Rebuild consequence

Free Apollo now has its own discovery, records, lists, evidence, draft writing, sequence planning, tasks, deals and cost ledger in one Worker and D1 database. It has no runtime dependency on Free Clay or Free CRM. The sequence editor mirrors step choices and planning controls, but the app has no mailbox sync, live sending, tracking, dialer, visitor identity or licensed Apollo contact database. [The feature map](FEATURES.md) and [comparison](APOLLO-COMPARISON.md) state these limits.
